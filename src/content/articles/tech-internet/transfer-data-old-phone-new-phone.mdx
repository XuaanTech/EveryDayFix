# How to Transfer Data from Old Phone to New Phone

Getting a new phone is exciting. Transferring everything from the old one is not. The process depends entirely on whether you're staying on the same platform (Android→Android, iPhone→iPhone) or switching (Android↔iPhone).

## Before You Start: Back Up Everything

**Do this first, even if you plan to use a transfer tool.** Things can go wrong.

### Android Backup
1. **Settings** → **Google** → **Backup**
2. Tap **Back up now**
3. Make sure **Backup to Google Drive** is ON
4. Verify what's being backed up: apps, contacts, settings, WiFi passwords

### iPhone Backup
1. **Settings** → **[Your Name]** → **iCloud**
2. Tap **iCloud Backup**
3. Tap **Back Up Now**
4. Wait for backup to complete (check the timestamp says "today")

> **Storage note:** iCloud gives you 5GB free. If your backup is larger, you may need to upgrade temporarily. Google Drive gives you 15GB free.

## Android to Android

This is the easiest transfer — Google has built it into the setup process.

### Method 1: During Setup (Recommended)
1. **Turn on your new Android phone** and start the setup process.
2. At the "Copy apps and data" screen, tap **Next**.
3. On your **old phone**, tap **Continue** when prompted.
4. **Choose how to connect:**
   - **Wireless:** Both phones connect to the same WiFi network.
   - **Cable:** Use a USB-C to USB-C cable (or USB-C to USB-A with adapter).
5. **Select what to transfer:** apps, contacts, messages, photos, device settings.
6. **Wait** — this can take 30 minutes to 2+ hours depending on data amount.
7. **Finish setup** on the new phone.

### Method 2: From Google Backup
If you already backed up to Google Drive:
1. During setup, select **Copy apps and data** → **Can't use old device?**
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. **Select the backup** you want to restore.
4. Apps download automatically (takes time — do it over WiFi).

> **What transfers:** Contacts, Calendar, Google Photos, app list (re-downloads), Chrome bookmarks, Gmail, device settings, WiFi passwords.

### What Doesn't Transfer
- Some app-specific data (game progress, app settings)
- Files stored locally (downloads, documents)
- SMS/MMS (use a third-party app like SMS Backup & Restore)
- DRM-protected content (movies, music you "rented")

## iPhone to iPhone

Apple makes this nearly seamless with Quick Start.

### Method 1: Quick Start (Recommended)
1. **Place both iPhones near each other** (within a few inches).
2. Turn on the **new iPhone** — it should detect the old one automatically.
3. A prompt appears on the old iPhone: **"Set Up New iPhone"** — tap **Continue**.
4. **Scan the animation** on the new iPhone using the old iPhone's camera.
5. Enter the old iPhone's **passcode** on the new one.
6. **Choose restore option:**
   - **Transfer from iPhone** (direct wireless — fastest)
   - **Download from iCloud** (if you prefer)
7. **Wait** — can take 30 min to 2+ hours.

### Method 2: iCloud Backup
1. **Back up old iPhone** to iCloud (Settings → iCloud → Backup → Back Up Now).
2. Turn on new iPhone → go through setup.
3. Choose **Restore from iCloud Backup**.
4. Sign in and select the backup.

### Method 3: Computer Backup (Most Thorough)
1. **Connect old iPhone** to Mac (Finder) or PC (iTunes).
2. **Back up** the iPhone locally.
3. **Connect new iPhone** to the same computer.
4. **Restore from backup** during setup.

> **Best for:** Large backups, or if you don't have enough iCloud storage.

### Method 4: iPhone to Android (Switching Teams)
1. On Android, during setup, select **Copy apps and data**.
2. On iPhone, install **Move to iOS** from the App Store.
3. On Android, tap **Continue** → enter the code shown on the iPhone.
4. **Select data to transfer:** contacts, messages, photos, videos, calendars, bookmarks.
5. **Wait** for transfer to complete.

> **Note:** Some iPhone apps don't have Android equivalents. The tool will show you what can and can't transfer.

## iPhone to Android (Switching Teams)

This is the trickiest transfer. Google's tool helps, but there are gaps.

### Step-by-Step
1. **Back up iPhone** to iCloud.
2. **On Android phone** during setup, select **Copy apps and data** → **Next**.
3. Select **Can't use old device?** → sign in with Google account.
4. Select **From iCloud** → enter Apple ID credentials.
5. **Select what to restore** from iCloud.
6. **Download Move to iOS** on your iPhone.
7. On Android, tap **Continue** → enter the code shown on iPhone.
8. **Select data to transfer** via Move to iOS.

### What Transfers Well
- Contacts (via iCloud sync or Move to iOS)
- Photos (via Google Photos or Move to iOS)
- Calendar (via iCloud or Move to iOS)
- Bookmarks (via Move to iOS)
- Messages (via Move to iOS — iMessage texts only)

### What Doesn't Transfer
- **WhatsApp messages** — need special handling (see below)
- **iMessage activation** — turn off iMessage on old iPhone before switching
- **Apple-specific apps** — no Android equivalent
- **Apple Music playlists** — use the Apple Music Android app or a third-party transfer tool
- **Health data** — Apple Health doesn't export to Android

## Transferring WhatsApp (Separate Process)

WhatsApp keeps its own backup system. It won't transfer with your regular phone backup.

### Android to Android
1. **Old phone:** Open WhatsApp → **Settings** → **Chats** → **Chat backup** → **Back up to Google Drive**.
2. **New phone:** Install WhatsApp, verify your number, and it will prompt you to **Restore from Google Drive**.

### iPhone to iPhone
1. **Old phone:** WhatsApp → **Settings** → **Chats** → **Chat backup** → **Back up to iCloud**.
2. **New phone:** Install WhatsApp, verify number → **Restore from iCloud**.

### Cross-Platform (Android↔iPhone)
WhatsApp now supports cross-platform transfer:
1. **Old phone:** Open WhatsApp → **Settings** → **Chats** → **Move chats to [Android/iPhone]**.
2. **Follow the prompts** on both devices.
3. Both phones need to be on the same WiFi network.

> **Important:** You must do this during initial WhatsApp setup on the new phone. If you already opened WhatsApp and skipped it, you'll need to reinstall.

## Special Cases

### eSIM Transfer
- **iPhone to iPhone:** Most carriers support eSIM transfer during Quick Start. Follow the on-screen prompts.
- **Android to Android:** Varies by carrier. May need to contact your carrier.
- **iPhone to Android or vice versa:** Usually need a physical SIM or carrier assistance.

### Two-Factor Authentication Apps
**These DON'T transfer automatically.** You need to manually re-setup:
- **Google Authenticator:** Use "Transfer accounts" feature before switching.
- **Authy:** Syncs automatically if you have an account.
- **Others:** Export recovery codes and re-add accounts on new phone.

### Banking & Payment Apps
- **Google Pay:** Sign in on new phone, re-add cards.
- **Apple Wallet:** Cards transfer with Quick Start, but verify they still work.
- **Banking apps:** May need re-verification (biometrics, OTP).

## Post-Transfer Checklist

After the transfer, verify everything:

| Check | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Contacts | Ensure all transferred (sync with Google/Apple) |
| Photos | Check cloud photos + local files |
| Messages | Texts, WhatsApp, other messaging apps |
| Apps | All re-downloaded and working |
| Passwords | Check saved passwords in browser |
| 2FA apps | Re-setup authenticator apps |
| Email | All accounts signed in |
| Calendar | All events transferred |
| Maps/Navigation | Saved places and home/work addresses |
| Music/Playlists | Check streaming apps have your playlists |

> **Do this check within 24 hours** while you still have access to the old phone.

## The Bottom Line

**Same platform (Android↔Android, iPhone↔iPhone):** Use the built-in transfer tools (Google's "Copy apps and data" or iPhone's Quick Start). They're fast and thorough.

**Cross-platform (Android↔iPhone):** Use Move to iOS + Google backup combo. It works but requires more manual steps for apps like WhatsApp and banking apps.

**Always back up first.** If anything goes wrong, you can restore from backup without losing data.
