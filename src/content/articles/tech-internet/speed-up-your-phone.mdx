# How to Speed Up a Slow Phone

A slow phone is one of the most frustrating everyday tech problems. Apps take forever to open, the screen freezes, and even simple tasks feel sluggish. The good news is that in most cases you can fix it yourself — without buying a new device.

Here are the most effective ways to speed up a slow phone, from quick fixes to deeper cleanup.

## The Quick Fixes

Start with these simple steps — they solve most slow-phone problems in a few minutes.

### 1. Free Up Storage Space

Low storage is the single most common cause of a slow phone. When your device is nearly full, it struggles to write temporary files, update apps, and even run the operating system smoothly.

- **Check your storage:** Go to Settings → Storage (the path varies by phone).
- **Delete unused apps:** Remove anything you haven't opened in months.
- **Clear out photos and videos:** Back them up to the cloud or a computer, then delete from the phone.
- **Remove large downloads:** Old PDFs, installers, and media files take up a lot of room.

> **A good rule of thumb:** Try to keep at least 10-15% of your storage free. This gives the system room to work.

### 2. Clear the Cache

Apps store temporary data (the "cache") to load faster. Over time, this accumulates and can slow things down.

- **Android:** Settings → Apps → select an app → Storage → Clear Cache.
- **iPhone:** There's no per-app cache clearing in iOS, but you can offload unused apps (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Offload App).

Clearing the cache is safe — apps simply rebuild it as you use them again.

### 3. Close Background Apps

Apps you're not using can still run in the background, consuming memory and CPU.

- Open the **recent-apps** view (swipe up and hold, or tap the square/card button).
- Swipe away apps you're done with, or tap **Close all**.
- Check **Settings → Battery** or **Settings → Apps** to see which apps use the most power in the background.

> **Note:** Force-closing every app constantly can sometimes make things worse, since reopening them uses more power. Close the ones you're not using, but don't obsess over it.

## Deeper Cleanup

If the quick fixes aren't enough, try these.

### 4. Update Your Software

Outdated operating systems and apps can have bugs and performance issues that newer versions fix.

- **Update the OS:** Settings → System → Software Update (path varies).
- **Update apps:** Open your app store and install pending updates.

Updates often include performance improvements and bug fixes that make a noticeable difference.

### 5. Disable Unused Apps

Some phones come with pre-installed apps you can't easily delete, but you can often **disable** them.

- **Android:** Settings → Apps → select the app → Disable.
- **iPhone:** You can't disable system apps, but you can offload or delete third-party ones.

Disabling an app stops it from running in the background and updating, which frees up resources.

### 6. Check for Problem Apps

Sometimes a single app is the culprit. Look for apps that:

- Crash frequently
- Use a lot of battery or data in the background
- Run constantly even when you're not using them

If you find one, update it first, then try clearing its cache. If problems persist, delete and reinstall it.

## The Last Resort: Factory Reset

If your phone is still slow after all the above, a **factory reset** can restore it to near-new performance. This removes everything — apps, accounts, cached data, and accumulated clutter.

> ⚠️ **Before you reset:** Back up your photos, contacts, messages, and anything else important. A factory reset erases all data on the device.

- **Android:** Settings → System → Reset → Erase All Data.
- **iPhone:** Settings → General → Transfer or Reset iPhone → Erase All Content and Settings.

After the reset, set up the phone as new (rather than restoring from a backup) for the biggest speed boost, then reinstall only the apps you actually use.

## How to Keep Your Phone Fast

Prevention is easier than a deep cleanup. Follow these habits:

- **Keep storage under control** — clean out photos and unused apps monthly.
- **Restart your phone weekly** — this clears temporary memory and fixes many small issues.
- **Update software regularly** — stay current with OS and app updates.
- **Don't install unnecessary apps** — each one takes space and runs background processes.
- **Watch your battery health** — a degraded battery can cause a phone to slow down to protect itself.

## When It's Time for a New Phone

If your phone is more than 4-5 years old and still slow after a factory reset, the hardware may simply be reaching its limits. At that point, a new phone (or at least a newer model) is often the more practical solution.

## The Bottom Line

Most slow phones can be fixed by **freeing up storage, clearing caches, closing background apps, and updating software**. Start with the quick fixes, and only consider a factory reset if those don't help.
