# How to Fix a Phone That Won't Charge

Your phone was charging fine yesterday, and today it won't charge at all — or it charges only when turned off, or only at certain cable angles. Before you panic about a broken phone, try these fixes in order. Most charging problems are simple to solve.

## Step 1: Try a Different Cable

Faulty cables are the #1 cause of charging problems, and they're the easiest fix.

1. **Use a different cable** — borrow one or try a spare.
2. **Try a different power adapter** too — the brick can fail independently of the cable.
3. **Try a different outlet** — the outlet itself might be the issue.

If a new cable works, the old one is damaged. Replace it — cables are cheap, and using a damaged one can damage your phone's charging port over time.

## Step 2: Clean the Charging Port

Lint, dust, and debris accumulate in the charging port over time — especially if you carry your phone in your pocket. This is the second most common cause.

1. **Look into the port** with a flashlight. You'll often see compressed lint.
2. **Use a wooden toothpick** (not metal) to gently scrape out debris.
3. **Alternatively, use compressed air** — blow a few short bursts into the port.
4. **Try charging again.**

> **Warning:** Never use metal objects (needles, safety pins, paper clips) to clean the port. Metal can short-circuit the charging contacts and cause permanent damage.

## Step 3: Restart Your Phone

A software glitch can sometimes prevent the charging circuit from activating.

1. **Power off the phone completely** — don't just put it to sleep.
2. **Wait 30 seconds.**
3. **Power it back on** and try charging.

## Step 4: Try Different Power Sources

Not all USB ports provide the same power:

- **Computer USB ports** provide limited power (0.5A) — many phones need 1-2A to charge.
- **Wall outlets** with a proper adapter provide the fastest, most reliable charge.
- **Try a different wall outlet** in case the outlet is faulty.

## Step 5: Check for Software Updates

Some charging issues are caused by software bugs:

- **Android:** Settings → System → Software Update.
- **iPhone:** Settings → General → Software Update.

Install any available updates and restart the phone.

## Step 6: Diagnose Intermittent Charging

If your phone charges only at certain angles:

1. **Wiggle the cable gently** at the port — if charging connects and disconnects, the port is likely loose or has worn contacts.
2. **Look for visible damage** inside the port — bent pins, dark spots, or corrosion.
3. **Try a different cable** — sometimes the cable connector is worn, not the port.
4. If the port is physically damaged, it needs **professional repair** (typically $50-100).

## Step 7: Wireless Charging as a Workaround

If the charging port is damaged, a wireless charger can keep your phone charged while you arrange repair.

1. **Check if your phone supports wireless charging** (most modern phones do).
2. **Place on a Qi-compatible wireless charger.**
3. This is a temporary workaround — get the port repaired for long-term reliability.

## Quick Diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Won't charge at all | Bad cable or adapter | Try different cable |
| Won't charge, but does when off | Software or port issue | Restart, update software |
| Charges intermittently | Loose port or bad cable | Clean port, try different cable |
| Charges very slowly | Low-power USB port | Use wall adapter |
| Gets hot while charging | Damaged cable or port | Stop using, replace cable |

## The Bottom Line

**Try a different cable first** — it's the most common cause and the easiest fix. If that doesn't work, **clean the charging port** with a wooden toothpick. These two steps solve 80% of phone charging problems. If the port is physically damaged, a wireless charger is a good temporary workaround while you get it repaired.
