# How to Extend Your Laptop Battery Life

Your laptop battery degrades in two ways: it drains faster per charge as you use it, and its total capacity shrinks over time. Good habits can dramatically slow both.

Most of the fixes are free — just settings changes and small behavioral tweaks.

## Part 1: Get More Hours Per Charge

These changes extend your daily battery life immediately.

### 1. Lower Screen Brightness

The display is the single biggest battery drain on any laptop. Reducing brightness from 100% to 60-70% can add 1-2 hours of battery life.

- **Windows:** Settings → System → Display → Brightness slider.
- **Mac:** Apple menu → System Settings → Displays → Brightness slider.
- **Enable adaptive brightness** so the screen adjusts automatically to ambient light.

### 2. Close Unused Browser Tabs

Every open browser tab consumes RAM and CPU cycles. Chrome is particularly aggressive.

- **Check tab count** — if you have 20+ tabs open, that's significant battery drain.
- **Use a tab suspender extension** (like The Great Suspender) to freeze inactive tabs.
- **Switch to a lighter browser** like Firefox or Edge if battery is critical.

### 3. Enable Battery Saver Mode

Both Windows and Mac have built-in power-saving modes that reduce background activity, visual effects, and CPU usage.

- **Windows:** Settings → System → Battery → Battery Saver. Set it to turn on at 20%.
- **Mac:** Apple menu → System Settings → Battery → Low Power Mode.

### 4. Turn Off What You're Not Using

- **Bluetooth:** Off when you're not using wireless headphones or a mouse.
- **WiFi:** Off when you don't need internet (Airplane mode is the quickest way).
- **Keyboard backlight:** Reduce or turn off in a bright environment.
- **Notifications:** Reduce background app activity by turning off non-essential notifications.

### 5. Unplug Peripherals

USB drives, external hard drives, webcams, and even charging phones draw power through your laptop's ports.

- **Unplug everything** you're not actively using.
- **Disconnect your phone** after it finishes charging.

## Part 2: Extend Battery Lifespan (Years)

These habits slow the long-term degradation of your battery.

### Charge Between 20-80%

Lithium-ion batteries degrade fastest at extreme charges (0% and 100%).

- **Don't let it drop to 0%** regularly — recharge around 20%.
- **Don't keep it at 100%** all the time — unplug once charged.
- The 20-80% range minimizes stress on battery cells.

### Avoid Extreme Heat

Heat is the #1 enemy of battery lifespan.

- **Don't use your laptop on soft surfaces** (beds, couches) that block ventilation.
- **Use a hard, flat surface** or a laptop cooling pad.
- **Don't leave your laptop in a hot car.**
- **Keep the vents clean** — dust buildup reduces cooling efficiency.

### Don't Keep It Plugged In 24/7

If you mostly use your laptop at a desk, the battery sits at 100% for days or weeks. This degrades capacity over time.

- **Unplug occasionally** and let it discharge to 20-30% before recharging.
- Some laptops have a "battery charge limit" feature in BIOS — set it to 80% for desktop use.

## Check Your Battery Health

- **Windows:** Open Command Prompt and type `powercfg /batteryreport`. This generates a detailed battery health report.
- **Mac:** Hold Option, click the Apple menu, and go to System Information → Power → Cycle Count and Condition.

If your battery health is below 80% of original capacity, it may be time for a replacement.

## The Bottom Line

For daily use: **lower brightness, close extra tabs, enable battery saver, and unplug peripherals.** For long-term health: **charge between 20-80%, avoid heat, and don't stay plugged in permanently.** These simple habits can add hours to your daily battery life and years to your battery's lifespan.
