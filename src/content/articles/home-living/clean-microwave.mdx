# How to Clean a Microwave in 5 Minutes

If your microwave looks like a crime scene from last week's spaghetti sauce explosion, you don't need to scrub for an hour. There's a trick that does 90% of the work for you: steam.

By microwaving water with lemon or vinegar, you create steam that softens every bit of grease, cheese, and dried food inside. After that, a quick wipe is all it takes.

## The Steam Cleaning Method

This is the fastest, easiest way to clean any microwave.

**What you'll need:**
- 1 cup of water
- 2 tablespoons of white vinegar OR juice from half a lemon
- A microwave-safe bowl
- A damp cloth or sponge

### Step 1: Make the Steam

1. **Fill a microwave-safe bowl** with 1 cup of water.
2. **Add vinegar or lemon juice.** Both work — lemon smells better, vinegar is slightly more effective on grease.
3. **Place the bowl** in the center of the microwave.

### Step 2: Steam It Up

1. **Microwave on high for 3-5 minutes.** You want the window to be completely fogged with steam.
2. **Don't open the door when it beeps.** Let it sit for another 2-3 minutes with the door closed. This is when the steam does its best work — loosening all the stuck-on food.

### Step 3: Wipe Clean

1. **Carefully remove the bowl** — the water will be very hot.
2. **Wipe the interior** with a damp cloth or sponge. Everything should come off effortlessly.
3. **Pay special attention to corners and the ceiling** of the microwave — these collect the most splatter.
4. **For stubborn spots,** dip your cloth back in the hot lemon water and give them a quick scrub.

That's it. Your microwave is clean.

## How to Clean the Turntable

The glass turntable and roller ring can be washed separately:

1. **Remove the turntable** and roller ring from the microwave.
2. **Wash in the sink** with warm, soapy water, or put them in the dishwasher (check your manual).
3. **Dry completely** before placing them back.

## How to Clean the Exterior

- **Standard finish:** Wipe with a damp cloth and mild dish soap.
- **Stainless steel:** Wipe in the direction of the grain with a damp microfiber cloth, then dry immediately to prevent water spots.
- **Control panel:** Spray a small amount of cleaner onto a cloth (never directly onto the panel) and wipe gently.

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't use bleach** inside the microwave — it can damage the interior surface and leave harmful residues.
- **Don't use abrasive scrubbers** (steel wool, harsh brushes) — they scratch the interior coating.
- **Don't spray cleaning products directly** onto the microwave walls — always apply to a cloth first.
- **Don't run the microwave empty** — this can damage the magnetron.

## Prevention Tips

- **Cover food** with a microwave-safe lid or paper towel when heating to prevent splatter.
- **Wipe up spills immediately** while they're still wet — it takes 5 seconds versus 5 minutes later.
- **Do a quick steam clean weekly** if you use the microwave daily — it prevents any buildup from forming.

## The Bottom Line

The **lemon or vinegar steam method** is the single best trick for cleaning a microwave. It takes 5 minutes, requires no scrubbing, and costs almost nothing. Keep a lemon or vinegar handy, and your microwave will never get gross again.
