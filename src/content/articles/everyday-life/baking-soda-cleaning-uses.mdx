# Baking Soda Cleaning: Uses You Didn't Know

Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) is a useful, inexpensive, and non-toxic cleaning product that many households already have in the pantry. While it won't replace every cleaning product you own, it handles a surprisingly wide range of tasks — from scrubbing ovens to deodorizing carpets.

Here are 12 surprising ways to use baking soda for cleaning — most of which you probably haven't tried.

## 1. Clean Your Oven (Without Harsh Chemicals)

The oven is one of the hardest things to clean, but baking soda can make the job more manageable.

1. **Remove oven racks** and set aside.
2. **Mix baking soda with water** to form a thick paste (about 3 parts baking soda to 1 part water).
3. **Spread the paste** all over the interior of the oven, avoiding the heating elements.
4. **Let it sit overnight** (at least 12 hours).
5. **Wipe clean** with a damp cloth the next day.
6. For stubborn spots, **spray with vinegar** — it will fizz, then wipe clean.
7. Clean the **racks separately** in the bathtub with hot water and baking soda.

## 2. Unclog Drains

Baking soda and vinegar is a popular, gentle approach for minor drain maintenance.

1. **Pour 1/2 cup of baking soda** down the drain.
2. **Follow with 1/2 cup of white vinegar.**
3. **Cover the drain** and wait 15-30 minutes.
4. **Flush with boiling water.**
5. For tough clogs, repeat or let the baking soda sit longer.

> This can help with minor clogs and is gentler on pipes than chemical drain cleaners. For severe or recurring clogs, a plunger or professional plumbing service may be needed.

> ⚠️ **Never pour boiling water down PVC or plastic pipes.** Many modern sink drains and the traps under them are PVC or other plastic, which can soften, warp, or crack under boiling water — potentially causing a leak or burst pipe. Use **hot tap water** instead of boiling water. If you're unsure what your drain pipes are made of, play it safe and skip the boiling water.

## 3. Freshen Carpets and Remove Stains

Baking soda can help lift stains and deodorize carpets.

1. **Blot the stain** with a clean cloth (don't rub).
2. **Sprinkle baking soda** generously over the stain.
3. **Let it sit** for 15-30 minutes (or overnight for tough stains).
4. **Vacuum thoroughly.**
5. For greasy stains, **mix baking soda with a small amount of dish soap** and apply as a paste first.

## 4. Clean Grout Between Tiles

Grout gets dirty and discolored over time.

1. **Make a paste** of baking soda and water.
2. **Apply to grout lines** with an old toothbrush.
3. **Spray with vinegar** for extra cleaning power.
4. **Scrub** and rinse with clean water.
5. This often helps brighten discolored grout, though results vary depending on how set-in the stains are.

## 5. Freshen Your Mattress

Mattresses absorb sweat, dust, and odors over time.

1. **Strip the bed** and vacuum the mattress.
2. **Sprinkle baking soda** generously over the entire surface.
3. **Add a few drops** of lavender essential oil for a fresh scent (optional).
4. **Let it sit** for at least 1 hour (overnight is better).
5. **Vacuum thoroughly** with an upholstery attachment.

## 6. Clean Stainless Steel Appliances

Baking soda can help remove fingerprints and streaks from stainless steel.

1. **Sprinkle baking soda** on a damp soft cloth.
2. **Gently wipe** in the direction of the grain.
3. **Rinse** with a clean damp cloth.
4. **Dry** with a microfiber cloth to prevent streaks.

## 7. Deodorize Your Refrigerator

An open box of baking soda in the fridge is classic for a reason.

1. **Place an open box** of baking soda on a shelf.
2. Replace it **every 30 days.**
3. For stronger odors, **sprinkle baking soda on a plate** and leave overnight.
4. To clean the interior: wipe shelves with a solution of **2 tablespoons baking soda per quart of warm water**.

## 8. Clean the Microwave

Steam with baking soda can help loosen hardened food residue.

1. **Mix 2 tablespoons of baking soda** with 1 cup of water in a microwave-safe bowl.
2. **Microwave for 5 minutes** until it boils and steams.
3. **Leave the door closed** for 3 more minutes (the steam loosens grime).
4. **Wipe clean** with a cloth — the steam should loosen most of the residue.

> ⚠️ **Superheating hazard:** When heating plain water in a microwave, it can become **superheated** — hotter than its boiling point — and erupt violently when disturbed or moved, causing serious burns. To reduce the risk: use a microwave-safe container with a lip or handle, **stir the liquid before and after heating**, and **avoid heating pure water for long periods without interruption**. Don't leave the microwave unattended while it's running.

## 9. Remove Clothing Stains

Baking soda helps lift stains from fabric.

- **Sweat stains:** Make a paste of baking soda and water, apply to the stain, let sit for 30 minutes, then wash.
- **Grease stains:** Sprinkle baking soda on fresh grease to absorb it, then brush off and wash.
- **Blood stains:** Soak in cold water with baking soda, then wash in cold water.

## 10. Clean and Deodorize Trash Cans

1. **Sprinkle baking soda** in the bottom of an empty trash can.
2. Let it **sit for a few hours** to absorb odors.
3. **Wash** with hot soapy water, then rinse.
4. Place a **small box of baking soda** at the bottom under the bag.

## 11. Polish Silver Jewelry

1. **Line a bowl** with aluminum foil, shiny side up.
2. **Add hot water** and 1 tablespoon of baking soda.
3. **Place silver items** in the solution, ensuring they touch the foil.
4. **Wait 2-5 minutes** — the tarnish transfers to the foil.
5. **Rinse** and dry with a soft cloth.

## 12. Clean Your Dishwasher

1. **Sprinkle 1 cup of baking soda** on the bottom of an empty dishwasher.
2. **Add 1 cup of white vinegar** to the bottom.
3. **Run a hot cycle** without any dishes.
4. This can help remove buildup and deodorize the machine.

## Baking Soda Cleaning Recipes

| Recipe | Ingredients | Use For |
|--------|------------|---------|
| All-purpose paste | 3 parts baking soda + 1 part water | Scrubbing sinks, tubs, tiles |
| Drain cleaner | 1/2 cup baking soda + 1/2 cup vinegar | Unclogging drains |
| Carpet freshener | 1 cup baking soda + 10 drops essential oil | Deodorizing carpets |
| Glass cleaner | 1 tbsp baking soda + 1 quart warm water | Windows, mirrors |
| Stain remover | Baking soda + dish soap paste | Fabric stains |

## Safety Notes

- Baking soda is **non-toxic and food-safe.**
- It's a **mild abrasive** — safe for most surfaces but avoid on delicate materials.
- Don't use on **aluminum, marble, or gold-plated items.**
- **Never mix baking soda with bleach** — it can produce harmful chlorine gas.
- ⚠️ **Never mix cleaning products unless the manufacturer specifically states they are safe to combine.** Mixing household cleaners (bleach + ammonia, bleach + vinegar, different drain openers) can produce toxic gases or dangerous reactions. Use one product at a time and rinse surfaces thoroughly before switching.

## The Bottom Line

Baking soda is a versatile and affordable addition to your cleaning routine. It handles tasks like oven scrubbing, carpet deodorizing, and stainless steel polishing — often at a lower cost than buying separate specialized products.

**Start with the oven clean and the drain maintenance** — two of the more practical everyday uses that many people find helpful.
